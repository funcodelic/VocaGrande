# VocaGrande roadmap

The big goals, in order. Detailed stages live in [BuildPlan.md](BuildPlan.md).

**Mission:** help adult learners build, measure, and keep a real vocabulary in Spanish and Italian, with a simple, calm app.

## Phase 1: Build the vocabulary
Add words and flesh them out: what the word is (language, part of speech, gender, meanings), then tally them. Quiz yourself with notecards. Ships with a free starter word list; AI features use your own API key.

## Phase 2: Speak it
Say words aloud and have the app recognize and tally them, entirely on the device. Adults who studied a language can find out how much they still remember, including words they hardly ever use.

## Phase 3: Organize by life, not classroom
Groups of related words (colors, seasons) and situations (travel, dating, meeting family, business). Filters for recently added, hardest, and easily confused words.

## Phase 4: Guided learning
AI-guided lessons that follow a logical path (pronouns, core verbs, basic sentences, then growth by topic), feeding the same repetition loop. Audio-first practice for walking, waiting, and driving.

## Phase 5: Learn together
Optional friends and shared goals: compete by word count, or work through a group or lesson together, such as preparing for an Italian club meeting. Opt-in and later, because it needs accounts and a server.

## Principles

- Minimal, simple, logical design; nothing game-show.
- Your vocabulary is yours; data stays on the device unless you choose otherwise.
- A word counts once, as a word, not once per form.
